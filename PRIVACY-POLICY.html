Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

AYAR Food Delivery ("we," "our," or "us") operates the AYAR mobile application (the "Service"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our food delivery service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account and use our food delivery service, we collect the following personal information:

  • Name - To identify you and personalize your experience
  • Email Address - For account creation, login, and order communication
  • Phone Number - For delivery coordination and customer support
  • Physical Delivery Address - To deliver food to your location
  • Precise Location Data (Latitude and Longitude) - To ensure accurate delivery to your exact location

📍 IMPORTANT: How We Use Your Location

Our app requires access to your device's precise location (GPS coordinates) for the following purposes:

  • Accurate Food Delivery: To ensure the delivery person can navigate to your exact doorstep
  • Show Nearby Restaurants: To display restaurants that deliver to your area
  • Real-Time Order Tracking: To show you the progress of your delivery on the map
  • Delivery Time Estimation: To calculate accurate delivery times based on distance

When We Access Location:

  • ✅ Only when the app is in use (foreground location)
  • ❌ We do NOT collect location data when the app is closed or running in the background
  • ✅ Location is only requested when you are placing an order or tracking delivery

Why This Matters:

Without precise location access, we cannot guarantee accurate delivery to your address. This may result in failed deliveries, delayed orders, and poor user experience.

1.2 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you access and use the Service:

  • Device information (device model, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • App usage statistics (features used, time spent, orders placed)
  • Crash data and performance metrics

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a backend-as-a-service provider. Supabase provides:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data is encrypted using SSL/HTTPS during transmission
  • Encryption at Rest: Data stored in Supabase databases is encrypted at rest
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database policies ensure users can only access their own data
  • GDPR Compliance: Supabase is GDPR-compliant and provides data processing agreements

Data Location: Supabase stores data in secure data centers located in the United States and European Union.

3.2 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide you with services
  • Required by applicable laws and regulations

You may request deletion of your data at any time by using the "Delete Account" feature in the app settings.

7. Account Deletion

7.1 In-App Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open the app and navigate to User Profile (tap the menu icon)
  2. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog
  4. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted

⚠️ Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

7.2 What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we delete:

  • ✅ User profile (name, email, phone)
  • ✅ Saved delivery addresses
  • ✅ Location data (latitude/longitude)
  • ✅ Feedback and reviews
  • ✅ Cart items
  • ✅ Notifications
  • ✅ OneSignal push token
  • ✅ Login credentials (auth user)

What Gets Preserved (Anonymized):

  • ⚠️ Order history - Preserved for admin and restaurant records, but anonymized (user_id set to NULL, customer name changed to "Deleted User")
  • ⚠️ Order items - Preserved as they are linked to orders and menu items for business records

Why We Preserve Orders:

  • Admin needs order history for business analytics and reporting
  • Restaurants need order records for accounting and tax purposes
  • Legal requirements may require keeping order records
  • Orders are anonymized - no longer linked to your personal information

Exceptions: We may retain certain data if required by law (e.g., tax records for completed orders).

7.3 Web-Based Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion via email at sarwari3.af@gmail.com

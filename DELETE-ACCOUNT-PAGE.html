🗑️ Delete My Account

AYAR Food Delivery - Account Deletion

Your privacy is important to us. You have the right to delete your account and personal data at any time.

Method 1: Delete In-App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the AYAR Food Delivery mobile app:

  1. Open the AYAR Food Delivery app on your device
  2. Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner
  3. Scroll down to "User Profile" section
  4. Tap the "Delete Account" button (red button)
  5. Read the confirmation dialog carefully
  6. Tap "Delete Account" to confirm
  7. Your account will be deleted immediately

✅ Instant Deletion

When you delete your account through the app, the deletion is processed immediately. You will be signed out automatically.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • ✅ User profile (name, email, phone number)
  • ✅ Saved delivery addresses
  • ✅ Location data (latitude/longitude history)
  • ✅ Feedback and reviews you submitted
  • ✅ Cart items (saved items in your cart)
  • ✅ Notifications (push notification history)
  • ✅ OneSignal push token
  • ✅ Login credentials (auth user account)

What Gets Preserved (Anonymized)

For legal and business record-keeping purposes, the following data is preserved but anonymized:

  • ⚠️ Order history - Preserved for admin and restaurant records, but anonymized (user_id set to NULL, customer name changed to "Deleted User")
  • ⚠️ Order items - Preserved as they are linked to orders and menu items for business records

🔒 Why We Preserve Orders

Order data is preserved but anonymized (no longer linked to your personal information) for:

  • Admin business analytics and reporting
  • Restaurant accounting and tax purposes
  • Legal record-keeping requirements

Your privacy is protected: Preserved orders cannot be linked back to you personally.

Processing Time

  • In-App Deletion: Immediate (instant)
  • Email Request: Within 30 days

Confirmation

After your account is deleted:

  • ✅ You will be automatically signed out of the app
  • ✅ You will not be able to log in with your credentials
  • ✅ For email requests: You will receive a confirmation email

⚠️ Account Deletion is Permanent

Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. If you want to use AYAR Food Delivery again, you will need to create a new account.
